How it works

For ongoing institutional purchasing, DocuFire supports purchase orders through Apple Business Manager and its Volume Purchase Program — the standard channel Apple provides for government and business buyers.
Good to know

A few honest notes

On Apple Business Manager

If your department doesn't already have an Apple Business Manager account, your IT team can enroll for free at business.apple.com. In the U.S., enrollment no longer requires a D-U-N-S number — a federal tax ID is enough.

On shared accounts

DocuFire syncs per iCloud account. If several investigators share one Apple ID across devices, they'll share the same case list rather than having separate private caseloads — which works well for a shared department pool, and we added a Lead Investigator field to each case specifically so it's still clear who's working what.
