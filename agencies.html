Path B

Purchase order, with IT

This uses DocuFire Institutional Edition: the same app, delivered through Apple Business Manager, which is Apple's free system for organizations that buy software. Your IT person will know it, or can set it up at business.apple.com in a few days.

01
Your IT person
Copy your Organization ID
Sign in to Apple Business Manager, open Preferences, and copy the Organization ID shown there.
02
You
Email it to us
Send the Organization ID and the number of iPads to support@docufire.app.
03
DocuFire
We send an invoice
$169.99 × the number of iPads. Pay it or issue a PO to DocuFire LLC the way you'd pay any vendor. Apple is not part of the transaction.
04
DocuFire
We switch the app on for your organization
Within about an hour, DocuFire Institutional Edition appears in your Apple Business Manager under Apps & Books → View Store → Custom Apps, showing $0.00 because you've paid us directly.
05
Your IT person
Take licenses and install
Open it there, choose Managed licenses, enter the number of iPads, click Get. Then install it on the iPads with the same tool they use for every other app (Jamf, Intune, Mosyle, Kandji, or similar).
06
Each investigator
Open DocuFire and sign in
Sign in with their Apple ID when asked. Done.
Path C

Purchase order, no IT and no Mac

Same as Path B through the invoice. The difference is how the app reaches the iPads: instead of a management tool, each investigator gets a code.

01
Enroll in Apple Business Manager
Free, at business.apple.com. Apple verifies you in a few days; a federal tax ID is enough. Then copy the Organization ID from Preferences.
02
Email us, pay the invoice
Send the Organization ID and the number of iPads to support@docufire.app. We invoice $169.99 × iPads and, once paid, switch the app on for your organization.
03
Get one code per iPad
In Apple Business Manager, open Apps & Books → View Store → Custom Apps → DocuFire Institutional Edition. Under License Type choose redeemable codes, enter the number of iPads, click Get.
04
Each investigator redeems their code
On the iPad: open the App Store app, tap the picture in the top corner, tap Redeem Gift Card or Code, enter it. DocuFire Institutional Edition installs like any app. Open it and sign in with their Apple ID when asked.
Good to know about codes

Each code works once and stays with the Apple ID that redeemed it. If an investigator leaves, their replacement needs a new code; email us and we'll sort it out.

Common questions

Straight answers

Where does our case data live?

In each investigator's own iCloud account, encrypted by Apple. Your Apple Business Manager account never holds case data, and neither does DocuFire LLC. We cannot see your cases.

What if investigators share iPads?

Put the shared iPads on one shared Apple ID. Everyone on it sees the same case list, and every case has a Lead Investigator field so it stays clear who's working what.

How do updates work?

Automatically, on every path. Nothing to re-buy or re-approve.

Can we add investigators later?

Yes. Path A: subscribe on the new iPad. Paths B and C: email us the new count; we invoice the added iPads, prorated to your renewal date, and your IT takes more licenses or codes.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes. Install the public DocuFire app from the App Store on one iPad and run a real case. Everything built there carries over when you move to Institutional Edition, because both use the same iCloud sync.

We already bought seats on the App Store. Can we move to a PO?

Yes. Email us; we'll time the switch so nobody loses access, and those investigators cancel their App Store subscription afterward.

Is there a desktop version?

Investigators can finish reports in a browser at portal.docufire.app, same Apple ID, same cases. Final PDF and Word export happens in the app. DocuFire also runs on Mac.

Who do we call?

Nobody, ideally. Email support@docufire.app and Roger Thorpe, who built DocuFire and is a working fire investigator, answers it himself.
