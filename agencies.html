Two ways to get DocuFire

Which one is you?

Individual investigator

Download DocuFire from the App Store, try it, and subscribe in the app. Nothing on this page applies to you.

Department, agency, or municipality

You need several seats, your finance office needs an invoice, and your IT team manages the iPads. That's DocuFire Institutional Edition, and this page explains exactly how it works.

What you get

Same app, different delivery

Institutional Edition is DocuFire with the subscription removed. Every feature is there: structure, vehicle, vessel, and wildland cases; scene diagrams and LiDAR room scans; witness statements; evidence log; PDF and Word reports; the desktop web portal at portal.docufire.app for finishing reports at the office. It arrives as a custom app in Apple Business Manager, which is Apple's standard channel for software that organizations buy rather than individuals. Your IT team installs it on investigators' iPads the same way they install anything else, and updates arrive automatically.

How it works

Five steps, start to finish

Two of these are ours, three are yours. The whole thing usually takes less time than the purchase order does.

01
Your department
Send us your Apple Business Manager Organization ID
Sign in to business.apple.com, open Preferences, and copy the Organization ID shown there. Email it to support@docufire.app with how many investigators you're equipping. If your department doesn't have Apple Business Manager yet, see the note below; it's free and takes a few days.
02
DocuFire
We send a quote
Per seat, for the term you want. You issue a PO or pay the invoice directly to DocuFire LLC through your normal procurement. Apple is not part of the transaction.
03
DocuFire
We unlock the app for your organization
When the PO is in hand, we add your Organization ID to the app's private distribution list. Within about an hour, DocuFire Institutional Edition appears in your Apple Business Manager store under Custom Apps. It shows a price of $0.00 there because you've already paid us.
04
Your IT team
Take licenses and push the app
In Apple Business Manager: Apps & Services → Apps & Books → View Store → Custom Apps. Open the app, take Managed licenses for the number of iPads, and click Get. Then assign it to the iPads through your MDM (Jamf, Intune, Mosyle, Kandji, or similar). A small department without an MDM can use Apple Configurator on a Mac.
05
Your investigators
Open the app and sign in
DocuFire opens like any app. When iOS asks, the investigator signs in with their Apple ID. Their cases are stored in their own iCloud account and sync to their own devices and to the web portal. Nothing to register with DocuFire.

What your IT team needs

Three things, all of which you may already have

An Apple Business Manager account

Free, at business.apple.com. Municipalities can use Apple Business Manager or Apple School Manager. Apple verifies the organization, usually within a few business days. In the U.S. a federal tax ID is enough; a D-U-N-S number is no longer required. Start this while the PO is in procurement.

A way to install managed apps

An MDM if you have one. If your IT team already manages iPads, you have this. For a handful of devices, Apple Configurator on a Mac does the same job over USB.

Your Organization ID

One line of text from Apple Business Manager's Preferences page. It's the only thing we need from you to turn the app on for your organization.

iPads

DocuFire is an iPad app. Any iPad running a current iOS works; iPad Pro adds LiDAR room scanning. It also runs on Mac. Your investigators' personal or department Apple IDs are fine either way.

Questions we get

Straight answers

How much does it cost?

Per seat, per year, quoted for your department. Email us with your investigator count and we'll send a number the same day. There's no setup fee, and the price you pay DocuFire is the whole price; Apple charges nothing on top.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The public DocuFire app on the App Store is the same software with a free trial. Have an investigator run a case or two on it. Whatever they build there stays in their iCloud and shows up in Institutional Edition when you switch, because both use the same sync.

Where is our case data stored?

In each investigator's own iCloud account, encrypted by Apple. Your Apple Business Manager account never holds case data, and neither does DocuFire LLC. We can't see your cases. If your department prefers a shared caseload, put the iPads on one shared Apple ID; every case carries a Lead Investigator field so it stays clear who's working what.

Do we need an MDM?

Not strictly. Custom apps from Apple Business Manager are installed through an MDM or through Apple Configurator on a Mac. Most departments with managed iPads already have an MDM; a small office can use Configurator and a USB cable.

How do updates work?

Automatically, through the same license. When we release a new version, it reaches your iPads the same way the first install did. Nothing to re-buy or re-approve.

Can we add investigators later?

Yes. Email us the new count, we adjust the invoice, your IT team takes more licenses in Apple Business Manager. No new procurement cycle for the app itself.

Can investigators work from a desktop?

Yes. The web portal at portal.docufire.app opens the same cases in a browser, using the same Apple ID as the iPad. It's built for finishing reports at the office; final PDF and Word export happens in the app.

We already bought DocuFire on the App Store for a few investigators. What now?

Keep using it until you're ready to move. When you switch to Institutional Edition, their cases come along automatically, and they cancel the App Store subscription. Email us and we'll time it so nobody loses access.
